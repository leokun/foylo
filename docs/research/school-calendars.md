# French school calendars: primary sources, zone mapping, and institution-specific closures

Research ticket: leokun/foylo#4 (part of #1). Researched on 2026-09-17 against primary sources only (Légifrance, data.education.gouv.fr, data.gouv.fr, geo.api.gouv.fr, Etalab source repositories).

## Question

Which primary source provides French school holiday calendars per zone, how often it changes, how a place (commune, académie) attaches to a zone, where public holidays (including Alsace-Moselle) come from, and how institution-specific bridge days and closures should be layered on top when no public source has them.

## Summary

- Use the Ministry's open dataset `fr-en-calendrier-scolaire` on data.education.gouv.fr (Licence Ouverte 2.0) as the machine-readable source for school holidays. It is the data behind the data.gouv.fr mirror and the per-zone ICS files. The legal source of truth remains the arrêté published in the Journal officiel; the dataset lags it.
- The dataset has 7 fields (description, population, start_date, end_date, location, zones, annee_scolaire). Dates are UTC datetimes that encode Europe/Paris midnights: start_date is 00:00 local on the arrêté's "fin des cours" day (a Saturday), end_date is 00:00 local on the return day (exclusive). Record shapes vary by year (single-point vs range records for Ascension and summer), so the ingestion layer must normalize.
- Zones: `Zone A`, `Zone B`, `Zone C`, `Corse`, plus one value per overseas territory. Zone composition is fixed in each arrêté and matches Code de l'éducation R222-2 (académie to département table). Place to zone therefore resolves as commune INSEE code -> département (geo.api.gouv.fr) -> académie (R222-2, static table of 30 rows) -> zone (arrêté). Corsica and overseas académies have their own calendars set by the recteur (D521-6).
- Update cadence is irregular: a triennial arrêté in Dec 2022 (2023-2026), then annual arrêtés in Oct 2025 (2026-2027) and Jul 2026 (2027-2028). As of today the dataset has 2026-2027 for metropolitan zones but not 2027-2028 even though the arrêté was published on 23 July 2026, so horizon is roughly 10 to 13 months and the dataset lag can exceed 8 weeks.
- Public holidays: Etalab's `jours-feries-en-france` dataset and `calendrier.api.gouv.fr/jours-feries/{zone}/{year}.json`, 13 zones including `alsace-moselle` (adds Vendredi saint and 26 December per Code du travail L3134-13), 20 years back and 5 years ahead. Vendredi saint is legally conditional on the commune having a Protestant or mixed church; the API applies it to the whole zone.
- Nothing public covers commune-level périscolaire (cantine, garderie) or crèche closures: these are optional local services whose rules live in each commune's or EAJE's règlement (Code de l'éducation L212-4, L551-1; Code de la santé publique R2324-30). They must be user-entered as Calendar closures layered above the zone calendar. The national Ascension bridge is in the arrêté and the dataset; other bridges (pré-rentrée, journées banalisées) are local.

## Findings

### 1. The legal source: arrêtés and the Code de l'éducation

- The national calendar is fixed by ministerial arrêté published in the Journal officiel. For 2026-2027: Arrêté du 22 octobre 2025 (JORF n°0250, 23 Oct 2025, NOR MENE2526965A), https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000052416058. For 2027-2028: Arrêté du 21 juillet 2026 (JORF n°0170, 23 Jul 2026, NOR MENE2618978A), https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000054457294. The previous arrêté was triennial: Arrêté du 7 décembre 2022 covering 2023-2024, 2024-2025 and 2025-2026 (JORF 8 Dec 2022), https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000046704476.
- Each arrêté contains: pré-rentrée des enseignants, rentrée des élèves, the four holiday periods per zone, summer start, the Ascension bridge ("les classes vaqueront le vendredi 7 mai 2027" for 2026-2027; 26 and 27 May 2028 for 2027-2028), and an article defining zone composition. Source: the two arrêtés above.
- Zone composition (identical in the 2022, 2025 and 2026 arrêtés): Zone A = Besançon, Bordeaux, Clermont-Ferrand, Dijon, Grenoble, Limoges, Lyon, Poitiers. Zone B = Aix-Marseille, Amiens, Lille, Nancy-Metz, Nantes, Nice, Normandie, Orléans-Tours, Reims, Rennes, Strasbourg. Zone C = Créteil, Montpellier, Paris, Toulouse, Versailles. Source: https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000052416058 and https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000054457294.
- Regulatory frame: Code de l'éducation D521-1 to D521-7 (Section 1, Aménagement du temps scolaire), https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006071191/LEGISCTA000020663220/. Key points: 36-week year in five periods separated by four holiday periods; recteurs may adapt the national calendar by arrêté for geographic or operational reasons (D521-1), within limits (D521-2, no holiday period reduced below eight days, changes limited to three consecutive days); recteurs of Corse, Guadeloupe, Guyane, Martinique, Mayotte and La Réunion may set three-year regional calendars (D521-6); for Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon the powers are exercised by the recteur of Normandie (D521-7). The arrêtés' article 5 explicitly sends Corse and the overseas académies to these articles.
- Consequence: Corsica is not a metropolitan zone. It has its own zone value and its own dates set by the recteur, even when they happen to coincide with Zone A (as they do for the four 2026-2027 holiday periods in the dataset).
- The human-readable page is https://www.education.gouv.fr/calendrier-scolaire-100148. Direct fetch returned HTTP 403 during this research, so no claim in this file relies on it.

### 2. The machine-readable source: dataset `fr-en-calendrier-scolaire`

- Dataset page: https://data.education.gouv.fr/explore/dataset/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire/information/. Metadata endpoint: https://data.education.gouv.fr/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire. Publisher "DNE - Ministère de l'Education Nationale", license "Licence Ouverte v2.0 (Etalab)", 2,423 records, metadata modified 2026-08-31. Description: official calendar published by the Ministry, with the conventions "Le départ en vacances a lieu après la classe des jours indiqués" and "La reprise des cours s'effectue le matin des jours indiqués" (quoted on the data.gouv.fr mirror, https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/le-calendrier-scolaire).
- Fields (from the metadata endpoint above):
  - `description` (text): name of the period, e.g. "Vacances de la Toussaint", "Pont de l'Ascension", "Début des Vacances d'Été". Observed distinct values also include overseas-only labels ("Vacances de Carnaval", "Vacances d'Été austral", "Journée banalisée", "Journée vaquée", "Pont", "Mi-carême", "Abolition de l'esclavage").
  - `population` (text): who is concerned. "-" for most metropolitan records; values like "Élèves", "Enseignants", "Élèves du premier degré", and overseas sub-territory labels ("Guadeloupe & Saint-Martin", "Saint-Barthélemy").
  - `start_date`, `end_date` (datetime, hourly precision, returned in UTC with `+00:00`).
  - `location` (text): académie name (one record per académie in a zone), or the territory name for Corse and overseas.
  - `zones` (text): `Zone A`, `Zone B`, `Zone C`, `Corse`, `Guadeloupe`, `Guyane`, `Martinique`, `Mayotte`, `Nouvelle Calédonie`, `Polynésie`, `Réunion`, `Saint Pierre et Miquelon`, `Wallis et Futuna`.
  - `annee_scolaire` (text): "2026-2027".
- Date semantics verified against the 2026-2027 arrêté (records queried via https://data.education.gouv.fr/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire/records?where=zones%3D%22Zone%20A%22%20and%20annee_scolaire%3D%222026-2027%22%20and%20location%3D%22Lyon%22):
  - Toussaint: `2026-10-16T22:00:00+00:00` to `2026-11-01T23:00:00+00:00`, i.e. Sat 17 Oct 00:00 Europe/Paris to Mon 2 Nov 00:00 Europe/Paris. The arrêté says fin des cours samedi 17 octobre, reprise lundi 2 novembre.
  - Hiver Zone A: `2027-02-12T23:00:00+00:00` to `2027-02-28T23:00:00+00:00` = Sat 13 Feb 00:00 to Mon 1 Mar 00:00, matching the arrêté (13 Feb to 1 Mar).
  - Rule: `start_date` = local midnight starting the arrêté's "fin des cours" Saturday; `end_date` = local midnight of the return day, exclusive. The UTC offset flips between +02:00 and +01:00 across DST, so consumers must convert to Europe/Paris before taking the date, never truncate the UTC string.
- Record shapes are not stable across years (same query for 2025-2026 vs 2026-2027):
  - 2025-2026 "Pont de l'Ascension" for Lyon is a range (`2026-05-13T22:00` to `2026-05-17T22:00`, Thu 14 to Mon 18 00:00), and summer appears as "Vacances d'Été" with two population rows (Élèves ending 1 Sep 00:00, Enseignants ending 31 Aug 00:00).
  - 2026-2027 "Pont de l'Ascension" is a single point (`start_date == end_date == 2027-05-06T22:00`, Fri 7 May 00:00) even though the arrêté closes Friday 7 May (and Saturday 8 May), and summer appears only as "Début des Vacances d'Été" with `start_date == end_date` and no return date. Corse 2026-2027 models the same bridge as a range (7 May to 10 May 00:00).
  - Consequence: the ingest layer needs a normalization step (point records = one day; derive summer end from the next year's rentrée or from the arrêté) rather than trusting `[start_date, end_date)` blindly.
- One row per académie: 2026-2027 has 48 rows for Zone A (8 académies x 6 periods), 66 for Zone B (11 x 6), 30 for Zone C (5 x 6), 6 for Corse. Historic rows keep pre-2020 académies `Caen` and `Rouen` under Zone B; they were merged into `Normandie` on 1 Jan 2020 by Décret n° 2019-1056 du 15 octobre 2019, https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000039229577. Deduplicate by `zones` if the app does not care about académie.
- Coverage and horizon (queried 2026-09-17): metropolitan zones and Corse have data through 2026-2027 only; 2027-2028 rows exist only for Mayotte (6) and Polynésie (10) although the metropolitan 2027-2028 arrêté was published on 23 July 2026. Overseas rows go as far as 2028-2029 (Polynésie, Mayotte). Query: https://data.education.gouv.fr/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire/records?where=annee_scolaire%3D%222027-2028%22&select=zones,count(*)&group_by=zones.
- Declared cadence: the data.gouv.fr mirror declares `frequency: annual`, temporal coverage to 2025-12-30 and last update 2026-08-31 (https://www.data.gouv.fr/api/1/datasets/le-calendrier-scolaire/). The static ICS files are updated per file; Zone-A.ics header says "Congés scolaires de la zone A depuis l'année 2017-2018", 68 all-day VEVENTs (`DTSTART;VALUE=DATE`), latest `DTSTART` 20270703, TZID Europe/Paris (https://fr.ftp.opendatasoft.com/openscol/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire/Zone-A.ics).
- Access paths (all Licence Ouverte 2.0):
  - Records API (Opendatasoft Explore v2.1, ODSQL `where`, `group_by`): `limit` max 100 and `offset + limit < 10000` (https://data.education.gouv.fr/api/explore/v2.1/swagger.json). 2,423 rows fit in a paged pull, or use exports.
  - Exports without row limit: https://data.education.gouv.fr/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire/exports/csv and `/exports/json` (listed as resources on data.gouv.fr).
  - Static ICS per zone: `https://fr.ftp.opendatasoft.com/openscol/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire/Zone-A.ics`, `Zone-B.ics`, `Zone-C.ics`, `Zone-A-B-C-Corse.ics`, `Corse.ics`, `Guadeloupe.ics`, `Guyane.ics`, `Martinique.ics`, `Mayotte.ics`, `NouvelleCaledonie.ics`, `Polynesie.ics`, `Reunion.ics`, `SaintPierreEtMiquelon.ics`, `WallisEtFutuna.ics` (resource list at https://www.data.gouv.fr/api/1/datasets/le-calendrier-scolaire/).

### 3. Mapping a place to a zone

- The arrêté maps académies to zones (section 1). Académies are defined by département in Code de l'éducation R222-2 (in force 1 Jan 2021), which lists 18 régions académiques, 30 académies and their départements, e.g. Lyon = Ain, Loire, Rhône; Corse = Corse-du-Sud, Haute-Corse; Normandie = Calvados, Eure, Manche, Orne, Seine-Maritime; Paris = Paris; Créteil = Seine-et-Marne, Seine-Saint-Denis, Val-de-Marne; Versailles = Yvelines, Essonne, Hauts-de-Seine, Val-d'Oise. https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042666480.
- Every metropolitan académie is a whole set of départements, so département code is sufficient to derive académie and zone. Corse départements 2A and 2B map to académie de Corse (own calendar). Départements 971, 972, 973, 974, 976 map to their overseas académies (own calendars).
- Commune to département: geo.api.gouv.fr Découpage administratif. `GET /communes/{codeInsee}`, `GET /communes?codePostal=...`, `GET /communes?lat=...&lon=...`, fields include `codeDepartement`, `departement`, `codeRegion`, `codesPostaux`. https://geo.api.gouv.fr/decoupage-administratif/communes. Postal codes can span communes and even départements, so prefer INSEE code or coordinates as the key.
- School to académie directly: dataset `fr-en-annuaire-education` (68,599 establishments, updated daily, Licence Ouverte 2.0) has `identifiant_de_l_etablissement` (UAI), `code_commune`, `code_postal`, `code_departement`, `code_academie`, `libelle_academie`, `type_etablissement`, `statut_public_prive`. https://data.education.gouv.fr/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/fr-en-annuaire-education. Useful if a Place is a school picked from a list: the académie comes for free. `code_academie` values also appear in `fr-en-contour-academies-2020` (30 rows, académie polygons), https://data.education.gouv.fr/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/fr-en-contour-academies-2020.
- The calendar dataset's `location` uses académie names, not codes, so a name join (or a maintained 30-row lookup table code -> name -> zone) is required. The zone-to-académie table changes only by arrêté; the last structural change was the Normandie merger in 2020.

### 4. Public holidays

- Legal list: Code du travail L3133-1 (11 holidays: 1er janvier, lundi de Pâques, 1er mai, 8 mai, Ascension, lundi de Pentecôte, 14 juillet, Assomption, Toussaint, 11 novembre, Noël), https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033020901. Alsace-Moselle (Bas-Rhin, Haut-Rhin, Moselle): L3134-13 adds "le Vendredi Saint dans les communes ayant un temple protestant ou une église mixte" and "le premier et le second jour de Noël", https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006902635.
- Open data: "Jours fériés en France" on data.gouv.fr, Licence Ouverte 2.0, published by data.gouv.fr (Etalab), https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/jours-feries-en-france/. Description: CSV/JSON cover 20 years back and 5 years ahead, ICS covers 5 years each way. Frequency field is "unknown" in the API metadata; the files are regenerated by a GitHub Actions cron from the `jours-feries-france` Python package (repo README at https://github.com/etalab/jours-feries-france-data, package at https://github.com/etalab/jours-feries-france).
- API: `https://calendrier.api.gouv.fr/jours-feries/{zone}.json` and `/{zone}/{annee}.json`, response is an object whose keys are ISO dates and values are holiday names. Zone enum: `alsace-moselle`, `guadeloupe`, `guyane`, `la-reunion`, `martinique`, `mayotte`, `metropole`, `nouvelle-caledonie`, `polynesie-francaise`, `saint-barthelemy`, `saint-martin`, `saint-pierre-et-miquelon`, `wallis-et-futuna`. OpenAPI: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/etalab/calendrier.api.gouv.fr/master/openapi.yml. Verified: `metropole.json` currently spans 2006-01-01 to 2031-12-25 (260 entries); `alsace-moselle/2027.json` returns 13 entries including `2027-03-26: Vendredi saint` and `2027-12-26: 2ème jour de Noël`.
- Caveats: (a) the API applies Vendredi saint to the whole `alsace-moselle` zone whereas the law conditions it on the commune; for a school-day model this is moot because schools are closed anyway across the three départements, but for an employer-side model it is an approximation. (b) Overseas zones add "Abolition de l'esclavage" on territory-specific dates (per the `jours-feries-france` README). (c) Schools are closed on lundi de Pentecôte because it is a legal holiday; the "journée de solidarité" affects employees, not pupils.
- Public holidays falling inside school holidays are redundant for pupils but still matter for a nounou or crèche contract, so keep them as a separate calendar layer rather than merging into the school calendar.

### 5. Institution-specific bridge days and closures: no public source

- What the national sources do cover: the Ascension bridge (in the arrêté and in the dataset as "Pont de l'Ascension"), the pré-rentrée (arrêté), and recteur-level adaptations for Corse and overseas (dataset rows such as "Journée banalisée", "Journée vaquée", "Pont"). Metropolitan académie-level adaptations under D521-1 are published as arrêtés rectoraux and are not in the dataset in a structured way beyond the per-académie rows.
- Cantine and garderie (périscolaire) are optional local public services. The commune has charge of public écoles (Code de l'éducation L212-4, https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000045293752). Périscolaire activities are organized by collectivités in a projet éducatif territorial (L551-1, https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000027682987). Canteens: communes for écoles, départements for collèges (L213-2), régions for lycées (L214-6), all optional; the organizing collectivité alone sets the règlement (https://www.collectivites-locales.gouv.fr/animer-les-territoires/services-la-population/les-services-publics-locaux/lenfance-et-lenseignement/la-restauration-scolaire). Closures (grève, pont local, travaux, fermeture estivale de la garderie) are therefore decided per commune, published in the règlement intérieur or the commune's notices, and exist in no national dataset. Checked catalog search on data.education.gouv.fr for académie/commune datasets (41 results): none describes périscolaire opening days.
- Crèches (EAJE): each establishment must have a règlement de fonctionnement specifying, among other items, "les horaires et les conditions d'arrivée et de départ des enfants" and admission rules (Code de la santé publique R2324-30, as amended by Décret n° 2021-1131, https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051418530). Annual closure weeks are set in that document per establishment; no aggregated open dataset exists.
- Nounou (assistante maternelle) closures are contractual between the family and the employee; by construction there is no public source.
- Layering model that follows from the above, for the Calendar entity in docs/02-domain-model.md:
  1. Base layer: zone calendar from `fr-en-calendrier-scolaire`, keyed by zone (or académie), school year, normalized to local-date intervals.
  2. Public holiday layer: from `calendrier.api.gouv.fr`, keyed by holiday zone (`metropole`, `alsace-moselle`, overseas) derived from the département.
  3. Institution layer: user-entered closures per Activity or Place (single days or ranges, with a label such as "pont", "fermeture annuelle", "grève"), plus user-entered openings that override the base (e.g. a garderie open during Toussaint, a crèche open on a school bridge day).
  4. Individual layer: ScheduleException per Person (already in the model).
  Precedence question to settle: an Activity-level "open" override must beat a zone-level "closed" (garderie during vacances), while a Person-level exception beats everything. School type matters too: a crèche does not follow school holidays at all, so the base layer must be optional per Activity.
- Operational notes: the dataset publishes the next year around 10 to 13 months ahead but has lagged the arrêté by more than 8 weeks (2027-2028 still missing on 2026-09-17), so the app should fall back gracefully (no data = no closures assumed, flagged to the user) and refresh the cache at least monthly. Because the arrêté is the legal source, a manual override of the zone calendar (or a second import from the arrêté text) is the only way to be correct in the lag window.

## Open questions for the grilling ticket

1. Zone attachment: attach a zone to a Place (derived from its commune) or to an Activity (a school explicitly picked from the annuaire, UAI-based)? A family can have children in two académies near a boundary.
2. Should the app store académie or only zone? Storing académie keeps the door open for recteur-level adaptations and avoids re-deriving when zone composition changes.
3. Normalization: which rule when `start_date == end_date` (point records)? One day, or look up the arrêté? Who owns the manual patch when the dataset is late or wrong?
4. Summer holidays: the 2026-2027 data has no summer end; derive from next year's rentrée (may be absent) or from a fixed convention (return day of `annee_scolaire + 1`)?
5. Refresh policy: monthly poll of the metadata `modified` field vs on-demand fetch when a date outside cached coverage is requested. What does the offline client hold: raw rows or normalized intervals per zone?
6. Precedence between layers: does an Activity-level explicit opening beat a zone closure? Does a public holiday beat an Activity opening (a crèche open on 11 November is unusual but legal)?
7. Crèches and nounous: opt out of the school base layer entirely, or model them as a Calendar with public holidays only plus manual closures?
8. Alsace-Moselle: apply Vendredi saint to the whole zone (API behaviour) or ask the user? For school-day purposes the whole-zone rule is correct; for contract hours it may not be.
9. Overseas and Corse: in V1 scope or explicitly excluded? The data exists, but the record shapes are richer (population splits, more description values) and would widen the normalizer.
10. License attribution: Licence Ouverte 2.0 requires mentioning the source and date of last update; where does that appear in the app?
